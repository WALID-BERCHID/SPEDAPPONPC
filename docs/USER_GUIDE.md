# Hand in Hand — How to use it

Hand in Hand is a **free** app for parents, teachers and specialists who support children with special needs.
It keeps everything about a child in one place and gives you tools to use with the child every day.
A free online community connects you with other families and volunteer specialists.

**Your child's data never leaves your computer.** It is locked with your password (AES-256 encryption).
There is no subscription and no advertising.

## 1. Install

1. Download **`HandInHand-x.x.x-Setup.exe`**.
2. Double-click it. Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* because the app is new and not yet code-signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Follow the installer. No administrator rights are needed. **Hand in Hand** appears in the Start menu.

Needs Windows 10 or 11.

## 2. First start

![Welcome](screenshots/01-welcome.png)

1. Type your name and choose **Parent / carer**, **Teacher / aide** or **Therapist / specialist**.
2. Pick your **country**. It sets the spelling (behavior / behaviour), the date format and the plan types (IEP, 504, EHCP, NDIS…).
3. Create a **password** (8 characters or more).

![Recovery key](screenshots/02-recovery-key.png)

Next you'll see your **recovery key**. It's the *only* way back in if you forget your password, so **print it or write it down**.

## 3. Finding your way

- **Sidebar (left):** everything, grouped into *Track*, *Support*, *Plan* and *Together*.
- **Child picker (top of the sidebar):** choose which child you're working with. Every page follows it.
- **Search (Ctrl + K):** jump to any page, child, goal, schedule, story or checklist by typing a few letters.

![Quick search](screenshots/33-quick-search.png)

- **"Saved and encrypted"** at the bottom of the sidebar means everything is safely stored.

## 4. Today

![Today](screenshots/03-today.png)

- The colored card shows the child, their strengths, active goals, behaviors this week (with an arrow showing whether they're going up or down) and their last feeling check-in.
- The big buttons are shortcuts to what you do most.
- **Coming up** lists appointments in the next 2 weeks. **Needs attention** lists goals with no data for 2 weeks.
- The latest home↔school message and your reward charts are shown too.

## 5. Children and "All About Me"

![Children](screenshots/04-children.png)

**Children → Add a child.** Only the first name is required. Teachers can see their whole caseload here.

![Profile](screenshots/05-child-profile.png)

Fill in strengths, interests, communication, sensory needs, triggers, what calms them, health and emergency contacts. Add a photo.

**All About Me page** makes a one-page summary for a new teacher, babysitter, substitute or hospital. Click **Print or save as PDF** (choose *Microsoft Print to PDF* to get a PDF).

![All About Me](screenshots/06-all-about-me.png)

## 6. Goals and progress

![Goals](screenshots/07-goals.png)

**New goal**: describe what the child will do, choose the area and the plan (IEP, 504, EHCP, NDIS, home goal…), and choose how you'll measure it:
- **% correct**: tap *Correct* or *Not yet* for each try.
- **Count**: tick *Lower is better* for things like meltdowns.
- **Minutes**: with a built-in stopwatch.
- **Rating 1–5**.

![Goal detail](screenshots/08-goal-detail.png)

Click a goal to record a session. You can also note how much **help was needed** (independent → full physical). The chart shows the target (orange dashed line) and the starting point.

## 7. Behavior log and patterns

![Behavior log](screenshots/09-behavior-log.png)

Answer three questions: **what happened before**, **what they did**, **what happened after**. Tap a suggestion or type your own, then add how strong it was, where it happened and how long it lasted.

![Patterns](screenshots/10-behavior-patterns.png)

**Patterns (last 30 days)** shows the busiest times, common triggers, days and places. It also shows the link to sleep if you record sleep in the notebook. *These are hints to discuss with your team, not conclusions.*

## 8. Daily notebook

![Notebook](screenshots/11-daily-notebook.png)

A home–school diary. Home and school each write an entry per day: mood, sleep, meals, toileting, medicine, what you did, good moments, concerns and a **message for the other side**.

## 9. Health

![Health](screenshots/12-health.png)

One tap to log **medicine**, **seizures** (with how long they lasted), **sleep**, **illness** or an **appointment**. Upcoming appointments also appear on the Today page.

## 10. Visual schedules

![Visual schedules](screenshots/13-visual-schedules.png)

Start from a template (morning routine, washing hands, first–then, bedtime) or make your own. Use icons or **your own photos**. Add minutes to a step to show a countdown.

![Show mode](screenshots/14-show-mode.png)

**Show** opens a full-screen view for the child. They tap each step when it's done: it gets a tick and is read aloud. **Press and hold "Hold to exit"** for 1.5 seconds to leave, so the child can't close it by accident.

## 11. Social stories

![Social stories](screenshots/15-social-stories.png)

Short picture stories that explain new or difficult situations. Templates include *Going to the dentist*, *Fire drill*, *When I feel angry* and *Getting a haircut*; change them to fit your child. Tap **Read** for a full-screen, page-by-page reader with read-aloud.

![Story reader](screenshots/16-story-reader.png)

## 12. Talking board

![Talking board](screenshots/17-talking-board.png)

Tap a picture to hear the word, and build short sentences like *"I want train"*. Press the blue speaker to say the whole sentence.
**Edit board** lets you add your own words and photos. The colors follow the usual communication-board key. You can make several boards (e.g. *Food*, *Feelings*). It uses the voices built into Windows, so no internet is needed.

## 13. Reward charts

![Reward charts](screenshots/18-reward-charts.png)

Pick one behavior, the number of tokens and a reward. Tap **Show**; the child taps a circle to add each token. When the chart is full, a celebration shows the reward.

![Reward chart](screenshots/19-reward-chart-show.png)

## 14. Visual timer

![Visual timer](screenshots/20-visual-timer.png)

The red part shrinks as time passes, and a gentle sound plays at the end.

## 15. Calm corner

![Calm corner](screenshots/21-calm-corner.png)

A full-screen calming space for the child:
- **Breathe**: a glowing circle grows and shrinks. *Breathe in… breathe out…*
- **How do I feel?**: the child taps a feeling, and it's saved to the child's record.
- **Calm sounds**: rain, ocean waves or a soft hum.
- **Count to 10**: tap to count together.

![Feelings](screenshots/22-calm-feelings.png)

## 16. Checklists

![Checklists](screenshots/23-checklists.png)

Ready-made checklists: before an IEP/plan meeting, starting a new school, doctor or dentist visits, sensory-friendly outings and a meltdown safety plan. You can edit them, tick items off, untick all to reuse a list, and print.

## 17. Reports for meetings

![Report](screenshots/24-report.png)

**Reports** builds a printable report for the last 30 days, 3 months, 6 months or everything. It covers the child's strengths, every goal (with chart and trend), a behavior summary, notebook highlights and space for notes and signatures.

## 18. Community

![Community](screenshots/25-community-landing.png)

A free space where parents, teachers and **verified volunteer specialists** help each other. Your child's private records are never uploaded; only what you choose to post is shared.

> **Until the community server is switched on**, click **Explore a preview** to try it with sample posts. Nothing is sent or saved in preview.

![Community home](screenshots/26-community-home.png)

- **Join** with your email. You'll get a 6-digit code, so there's no password to remember. Choose the name others will see.
- **Home**: latest discussions, filtered by group or searched. Click **New post** to write one.
- **Groups**: *Just diagnosed*, *School, IEPs & EHCPs*, *Behavior & meltdowns*, *Speech & communication*, *Sensory*, *Daily life*, *Teachers' lounge*, *Teens & transition*, *Caring for yourself*.

![Post](screenshots/27-community-post.png)

- On a post, tap **Helpful** on replies that helped. If you asked the question, **Mark as most helpful** on the best answer.
- **Before you post**, the app checks for your child's name, school, phone numbers or emails and warns you.
- **Report** anything unkind, unsafe or that shares a child's details; a moderator will review it.

![Ask a specialist](screenshots/28-community-ask.png)

**Ask a specialist**: ask a question that verified SLPs, OTs, BCBAs, psychologists and teachers answer for free.

![Library](screenshots/29-community-library.png)

**Library**: schedules, stories, talking boards and checklists shared by others. **Add to [child]** imports one into your app in one click. **Share from my app** shares yours; photos are never shared, only icons and words.

![Live sessions](screenshots/30-community-events.png)

**Live sessions**: free online Q&As and workshops. Click **I'll join** to reserve a place. Verified specialists and members at the *Guide* level can host.

## 19. Points, rewards and certificates

![Rewards](screenshots/31-community-rewards.png)

People who help are recognized, never paid:

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Your answer is marked most helpful | +15 |
| A "helpful" vote on your reply | +2 |
| A verified specialist answers a question | +5 |
| Replying to someone | +1 |
| Sharing a template | +3 |
| Every 10 people who import your template | +5 |
| Hosting a confirmed live session | +50 |

- **Levels**: 🌱 Seed (0 points) → 🤝 Helper (50) → 🧭 Guide (200) → 🌟 Mentor (500) → 🏛️ Pillar (1,000). Each level unlocks something new.
- **Badges**: *First words*, *Helping hand*, *Lifesaver*, *Crowd favorite*, *Template maker*, *Session host*, *Verified specialist*, *Pillar*.
- To stay fair, points mostly come from other people's votes, with a daily limit.
- **Specialists** can ask to be verified (profession and registration number), and an admin checks the official register.

![Certificate](screenshots/32-certificate.png)

From the **Helper** level you can **Get certificate**: a printable *Certificate of Contribution* with your points, answers, helpful votes, sessions and volunteer hours.
Anyone can scan the **QR code** or enter the ID on the verification page to confirm it's real. It records volunteer work; it isn't a professional qualification.

## 20. Settings, backup and sharing

![Settings](screenshots/34-settings.png)

- **Appearance**: Auto, Light, **Dark** or **High contrast**; text size; an extra-readable font; reduced motion.

![Dark mode](screenshots/35-dark-mode.png)

- **Lock automatically** after some minutes without use. **Change password** or make a **new recovery key**.
- **Backups:** the app saves one automatically every day (the last 10 are kept). Use **Save a backup** to copy one to a USB stick or your own OneDrive/Google Drive folder.
- **Sharing between home and school (no internet needed):** on the child's profile, click **Share file** and choose a share password. Send the file by email or USB, and give the password separately. The other person uses **Settings → Import a share file**.

![Lock](screenshots/36-lock-screen.png)

Click **Lock** (bottom left) when you step away. Forgot your password? Use **"Forgot your password? Use your recovery key"**.

## 21. Questions

- **Is it really free?** Yes, all of it. If you want to help, use **Support this project**.
- **Is it a medical device?** No. It helps you organise information and supports your child day to day; always follow professional advice.
- **Can two computers share data?** Yes, with share files (section 20).
- **Is the community safe?** It's for adults only, specialists are verified, there are moderators and report buttons, and the app checks your posts for private details. Never post children's names, photos or schools.
